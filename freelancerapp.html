04. Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working together, timelines, and project handover.

What is your typical project turnaround time?
Most full product MVPs and design system overhauls take between 4 to 7 weeks from kick-off meeting to final production deployment, depending on scope complexity.
How are payments and milestones structured?
Engagements operate on a fixed-fee milestone structure: 50% upfront deposit to secure the spot in the studio calendar, 25% upon design system approval, and 25% upon final code handover.
Do you hand over clean code and Figma files?
Yes. You receive full ownership rights to all tokenized Figma files, production-ready React/TypeScript repositories, and component documentation upon final completion.
Can you work with our existing engineering team?
Absolutely. I frequently operate as a fractional design engineering lead, embedding directly into your GitHub and Slack workflows to guide your internal devs.
